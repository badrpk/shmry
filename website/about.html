Our Story

Founded in 2024, SHMRY emerged from the recognition that while AI and cloud technologies were advancing rapidly, they remained inaccessible to many developers and small businesses due to complexity, cost, and infrastructure limitations.

We started with a simple idea: what if we could create a platform that combines the power of edge computing with the simplicity of modern web development? The result is SHMRY - a comprehensive platform that brings together:

  • 🤖 Advanced AI models and inference engines
  • ☁️ Distributed cloud computing infrastructure
  • 📧 Professional email and communication tools
  • 🌐 Website and application development platforms
  • ⚡ Edge computing for low-latency performance
