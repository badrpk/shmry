🎯 Call Center Platform

Complete customer service and support platform with AI-powered automation, omnichannel communication, and advanced analytics.

Overview

SHMRY Call Center Platform provides enterprise-grade customer service solutions that streamline communication, automate routine tasks, and deliver exceptional customer experiences across all channels.

📞 Omnichannel Support

Voice, chat, email, and social media in one platform.

🤖 AI Automation

Intelligent routing, chatbots, and self-service options.

📊 Advanced Analytics

Real-time insights and performance metrics.

🔒 Enterprise Security

Compliance, encryption, and access controls.

Features

Communication Channels

  • Voice Calls: HD audio, call recording, and advanced routing
  • Live Chat: Real-time messaging with file sharing
  • Email Support: Ticket management and automated responses
  • Social Media: Monitor and respond to social mentions
  • SMS/WhatsApp: Text messaging and notifications

AI & Automation

  • Intelligent Routing: AI-powered call and ticket distribution
  • Chatbots: Automated responses and self-service
  • Sentiment Analysis: Monitor customer satisfaction in real-time
  • Predictive Analytics: Forecast call volumes and staffing needs
  • Voice Recognition: Speech-to-text and transcription

Management Tools

  • Agent Dashboard: Unified interface for all communications
  • Performance Metrics: Track KPIs and agent productivity
  • Quality Assurance: Call monitoring and scoring
  • Training Modules: Interactive learning and certification
  • Workforce Management: Scheduling and capacity planning
