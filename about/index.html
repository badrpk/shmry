Our Mission

To democratize AI and cloud computing by creating a distributed, edge-powered platform that makes advanced technology accessible to everyone, everywhere.

Our Story

The Beginning

SHMRY was born from a simple observation: the future of computing isn't in massive, centralized data centers, but in a distributed network of edge devices that bring AI and cloud capabilities closer to users.

We started with a vision of making AI inference, vector databases, and cloud computing accessible to developers and businesses of all sizes, regardless of their location or infrastructure.
