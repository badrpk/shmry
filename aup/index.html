Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

This policy outlines the acceptable use of Shmry's platform and services. By using our services, you agree to comply with these guidelines.

1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) governs the use of Shmry's platform, services, and infrastructure. It is designed to ensure a safe, secure, and productive environment for all users while protecting our systems and other users.

2. Acceptable Use

Shmry services may be used for lawful purposes only. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of our services complies with all applicable laws and regulations.

2.1 Permitted Uses

Our services are designed for:

  • AI and machine learning applications
  • Data processing and analytics
  • Web and mobile application hosting
  • Business and enterprise applications
  • Research and development projects
  • Educational and non-profit initiatives

3. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on our platform:

3.1 Illegal Activities

  • Any activity that violates applicable laws or regulations
  • Infringement of intellectual property rights
  • Distribution of illegal content or materials
  • Fraudulent activities or scams
  • Money laundering or financial crimes

3.2 Harmful Content

  • Malware, viruses, or other harmful code
  • Phishing attempts or social engineering
  • Content that promotes violence or harm
  • Hate speech or discriminatory content
  • Explicit adult content without proper age verification

3.3 System Abuse

  • Attempting to gain unauthorized access to systems
  • Denial of service attacks or network flooding
  • Exploiting security vulnerabilities
  • Interfering with other users' services
  • Excessive resource consumption beyond allocated limits

3.4 Spam and Unsolicited Communications

  • Bulk unsolicited emails or messages
  • Harvesting email addresses or personal information
  • Impersonating others or misrepresenting identity
  • Commercial solicitation without consent

4. AI and Content Generation

When using our AI services, users must ensure:

  • Generated content complies with this policy
  • AI models are not used to create harmful or deceptive content
  • Generated content respects intellectual property rights
  • AI services are not used for automated harassment

5. Data and Privacy

Users must comply with data protection requirements:

  • Obtain proper consent for data collection
  • Implement appropriate security measures
  • Respect data subject rights
  • Comply with applicable privacy laws
  • Not process sensitive data without proper safeguards

6. Resource Usage

Users must use resources responsibly:

  • Stay within allocated quotas and limits
  • Not intentionally waste or abuse resources
  • Use appropriate instance types for workloads
  • Implement proper resource management

7. Monitoring and Enforcement

Shmry reserves the right to:

  • Monitor service usage for policy compliance
  • Investigate suspected violations
  • Take appropriate action against violations
  • Cooperate with law enforcement when required

8. Consequences of Violations

Violations of this policy may result in:

  • Warning notifications
  • Temporary service suspension
  • Permanent account termination
  • Legal action if necessary
  • Reporting to appropriate authorities

9. Reporting Violations

To report violations of this policy:

  • Email: abuse@shmry.com
  • Security Issues: security@shmry.com
  • Legal Matters: legal@shmry.com

10. Updates to Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes through:

  • Email notifications
  • Platform announcements
  • Updates to our website
