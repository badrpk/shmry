Service Level Agreement (SLA)

This SLA defines Shmry's commitment to service availability, performance, and support response times for our platform services.

1. Service Availability

Shmry commits to maintaining high service availability across all our platform services. Our availability targets are measured monthly and apply to all production services.

1.1 Monthly Uptime Percentage

Service Tier Monthly Uptime Maximum Monthly Downtime
Free 99.0% 7.2 hours
Builder 99.5% 3.6 hours
Scale 99.9% 43.2 minutes
Enterprise 99.99% 4.32 minutes

1.2 Service Credits

If we fail to meet our uptime commitment, customers are eligible for service credits based on the following schedule:

Monthly Uptime Service Credit
99.0% - 99.9% 10% of monthly bill
95.0% - 99.0% 25% of monthly bill
90.0% - 95.0% 50% of monthly bill
Below 90.0% 100% of monthly bill

2. Performance Standards

Shmry maintains performance standards for key services to ensure optimal user experience.

2.1 API Response Times

  • AI Inference: 95% of requests under 2 seconds
  • Vector Search: 95% of queries under 100ms
  • File Upload: 95% of uploads under 5 seconds (per MB)
  • Authentication: 95% of requests under 500ms

2.2 Data Processing

  • Vector Indexing: 95% of operations under 1 second
  • Model Loading: 95% of models load within 30 seconds
  • Batch Processing: 95% of batches complete within SLA

3. Support Response Times

We provide different support response times based on issue severity and customer tier.

3.1 Issue Severity Levels

  • Critical (P1): Service completely unavailable or major security breach
  • High (P2): Significant performance degradation or partial service outage
  • Medium (P3): Minor performance issues or feature limitations
  • Low (P4): General questions, feature requests, or documentation

3.2 Response Time Commitments

Customer Tier Critical (P1) High (P2) Medium (P3) Low (P4)
Free 4 hours 24 hours 72 hours 1 week
Builder 2 hours 12 hours 48 hours 1 week
Scale 1 hour 6 hours 24 hours 3 days
Enterprise 30 minutes 2 hours 8 hours 24 hours

4. Maintenance Windows

Shmry schedules regular maintenance to ensure platform stability and security.

4.1 Planned Maintenance

  • Frequency: Monthly on the first Sunday
  • Duration: Maximum 4 hours
  • Notification: 7 days advance notice
  • Impact: Minimal to no service interruption

4.2 Emergency Maintenance

  • Notification: 2 hours advance notice when possible
  • Duration: As short as possible
  • Communication: Real-time updates via status page

5. Data Protection and Recovery

We maintain robust data protection and recovery procedures to safeguard customer data.

5.1 Backup and Recovery

  • Backup Frequency: Daily automated backups
  • Retention: 30 days for standard backups, 1 year for monthly
    • Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 4 hours for critical data
  • Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 24 hours maximum data loss

5.2 Disaster Recovery

  • Geographic Redundancy: Multi-region deployment
    • Failover Time: Automatic failover within 15 minutes
  • Business Continuity: 99.9% uptime during disaster scenarios

6. Security and Compliance

Shmry maintains comprehensive security measures and compliance certifications.

6.1 Security Standards

  • Encryption: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit
  • Access Control: Multi-factor authentication, role-based access
  • Monitoring: 24/7 security monitoring and threat detection
  • Incident Response: 1-hour response to security incidents

6.2 Compliance

  • Certifications: ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II
  • Standards: GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA compliance
  • Audits: Annual third-party security audits

7. Service Credits and Refunds

Service credits are automatically applied to customer accounts when SLA commitments are not met.

7.1 Credit Calculation

  • Credits are calculated based on the affected service period
  • Maximum credit per month is 100% of monthly bill
  • Credits must be used within 12 months
  • Credits are not transferable or refundable

7.2 Credit Request Process

  • Credits are automatically calculated and applied
  • Customers can request manual review within 30 days
  • Disputes are resolved within 5 business days

8. Exclusions

The following situations are excluded from SLA commitments:

  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, government actions)
  • Customer-caused issues or misconfigurations
  • Third-party service dependencies
  • Scheduled maintenance windows
  • Beta or preview services
  • Services used in violation of Terms of Service

9. Monitoring and Reporting

Shmry provides comprehensive monitoring and reporting on service performance.

9.1 Real-time Monitoring

  • Status Page: Real-time service status updates
  • Metrics Dashboard: Performance and availability metrics
  • Alerting: Proactive notification of issues

9.2 Monthly Reports

  • Uptime Report: Monthly availability statistics
    • Performance Report: Response time and throughput metrics
  • Incident Report: Summary of major incidents and resolutions
