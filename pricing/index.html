Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I exceed my plan limits?
You'll be charged overage fees based on the rates shown above. We'll notify you when you reach 80% of your limits so you can upgrade or adjust usage.
Can I change plans at any time?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate your billing accordingly.
Is there a long-term contract?
No contracts required. All plans are month-to-month with the option to cancel at any time. Enterprise customers can choose annual billing for discounts.
What's included in "unlimited everything" for Enterprise?
Enterprise plans include unlimited AI inferences, vector operations, storage, and projects. We work with you to set reasonable usage expectations and provide dedicated infrastructure.
Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes! Contact our sales team for custom pricing on high-volume usage. We offer significant discounts for large-scale deployments.
