How It Works

Full Device Surrender

  • Shmry manages device optimally for maximum resource utilization
  • Higher revenue potential due to efficient resource allocation
  • Automated maintenance and priority task allocation
  • Subscribers earn 90% of revenue generated by their device

Customized Allocation

  • Subscribers control exactly how much computing power and storage to share
  • Flexible scheduling and selective task acceptance
  • Personal device control while still earning revenue
  • Manual maintenance control with Shmry support

Revenue Sharing Model

  • 90% of revenue goes directly to device owners
  • 10% covers platform costs, maintenance, and development
  • Real-time earnings tracking and automated payouts
  • Monthly and lifetime earnings statistics
