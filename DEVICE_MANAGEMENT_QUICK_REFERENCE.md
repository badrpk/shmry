## 💰 Revenue Sharing

- **Device Owners**: 90% of earnings
- **Platform**: 10% (costs & development)

### Earnings Example
```
8 vCPU + 16 GB RAM + 1 TB Storage = $31/month
Yearly: $372 × 0.90 = $334.80 to device owner
```
